# XEEKIN — Anyone Can Be a Performer (2025)

XEEKIN is an interactive media performance designed so anyone can stand center stage. The spotlight turns on the moment you participate—building a “performance for everyone.”

It fuses game-like fun, musical rhythm, real-time visuals, and performance intensity. Audiences move the space, play, and become the protagonist.

**Role**: Design / Dev  
**Tools**: TouchDesigner, GLSL, OSC  
**Year**: 2025
